Rock assets used by the ocean background
=========================================

Files
-----
- cliff_c.glb      - Cliff C, converted from C_Cliff_CM.obj
- rock_d.glb       - Rock Formation D, converted from D_Rock_Formation_CM.obj
- tall_rock.glb    - TallRock_2.glb, supplied by the plugin owner

Source
------
Cliff C and Rock Formation D come from the free "FREE Cliffs and Rock
Formations" pack supplied by the plugin owner.

TallRock_2.glb and its texture set also come from the plugin owner.

Processing
----------
- Cliff C was decimated from about 41k to about 14k triangles.
- All textures were reduced to 1024 px and embedded in the GLB files.
- Materials were rebuilt with the supplied base colour, normal and roughness
  maps; the height maps were not used at runtime.
